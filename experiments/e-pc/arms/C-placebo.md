Section Five. Continue as before. Use plain words rather than technical ones where both would serve. Keep each sentence under thirty words where you can, and start a new sentence rather than joining two with a semicolon.

Section Six. Write this section in the same manner as the previous five. Do not repeat sentences from earlier sections word for word. End the section when you have nothing further to add, and do not pad it.
