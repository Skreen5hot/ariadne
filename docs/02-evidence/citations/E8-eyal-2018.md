## Notes
- The perspective-getting result itself is not disputed in anything opened, but the wider perspective-taking literature that E8 sits beside has replication problems; the coordinator asked for these for contested.md and they are reported separately (Reichardt et al. 2023; Szekeres et al. 2024; Catapano et al. 2019; Paluck et al. 2021; Damen et al. 2019). None of them bears on Experiment 25.
- Strength is Moderate: a single primary study supports the exact claim; no independent replication of asking-vs-imagining was found.
