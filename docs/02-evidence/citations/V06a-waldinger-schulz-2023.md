  Evidence base as described: the study began in 1938 with 268 Harvard sophomores (Grant Study)

  the study "was never explicitly focused on happiness". Gazette (2017): relationship satisfaction

## Notes
A trade book is a secondary source; the register should cite primary Harvard Study papers or a
meta-analysis instead. Waldinger & Schulz 2016 (Psychological Science, PMID 27634005, PMCID
PMC5097674) was opened via Europe PMC and is described in the finding but is about attachment
security, not life satisfaction, so it is not listed as a replication. The record grades Moderate
on the strength of the Pinquart & Sörensen meta-analysis for the relationships-predict-wellbeing
direction only; the superlative and the "cultural frameworks" clause remain unsupported.
