## Notes
ISRN Psychiatry was a Hindawi journal later folded into International Scholarly Research Notices; the
article remains indexed in PMC. The review is a serious summary of a very large literature, but it is
the wrong instrument for the specific "independent of religious belief" claim, by its own definitional
choice. For the ratifier: the v1 sentence bundles two separate claims (practice-without-belief, and
transformative transcendent experience); they should probably be split.
