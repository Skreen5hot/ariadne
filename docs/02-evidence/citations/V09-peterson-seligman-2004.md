fit_note: |
  What the handbook supports is "positive traits can be classified into 24 measurable character strengths
  grouped under six virtues". It is not the source of evidence that virtue "strongly predicts wellbeing",
  and in particular not for gratitude, kindness and integrity. The data source is Park, Peterson &
  Seligman 2004 (V09x), and there the strongest correlates of life satisfaction are hope, zest, gratitude,
  love and curiosity, with kindness and integrity among the weaker ones. The v1 sentence therefore names
  one strength the data support (gratitude) and two it does not (kindness, integrity).
