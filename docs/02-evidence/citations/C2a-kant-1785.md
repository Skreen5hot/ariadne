Abbott translation, Second Section (kingdom-of-ends discussion): everything has either value (price) or dignity; what has a value can be replaced by an equivalent; what is above all value and admits of no equivalent has dignity. Kant then distinguishes market value (skill, diligence), fancy value (wit, humour) and intrinsic worth, i.e. dignity (morality, and humanity as capable of it), and says dignity is placed infinitely above all value and cannot be brought into comparison or competition without violating its sanctity. German locus confirmed on korpora.org Ak. 4:434, lines 31-34 ("Was einen Preis hat ... das hat eine Wuerde"); the korpora page shows the sentence complete on 434.
