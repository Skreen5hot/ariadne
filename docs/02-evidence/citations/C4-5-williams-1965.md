  On the term "remainder": Williams does use the word. van Domselaar (Jurisprudence 13(2), 2022, pp. 220-239, DOI 10.1080/20403313.2021.2014709, opened as the UvA repository PDF) quotes Williams asserting that moral conflicts are "neither systematically avoidable, nor all soluble without remainder", citing Problems of the Self p. 179, and says Williams "introduced the concept of moral remainder" there. The SEP Williams entry (rev. 2023) quotes PS pp. 183-4 to the effect that it falsifies moral thought to represent its logic as demanding that in a conflict one of the conflicting oughts must be totally rejected, and describes the residue as regret (later "agent-regret", Moral Luck 1981). The SEP Moral Dilemmas entry uses the term "moral residue" and names Williams (1965) and Marcus (1980) as the best-known proponents of arguments for dilemmas from residue. So "remainder" is Williams's word (PS p. 179) though "moral residue" and "moral remainder" as labels are largely the secondary literature's.
