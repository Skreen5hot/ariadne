## Notes
For the register claim as worded ("multidimensional", not "six-dimensional") the critics and Ryff agree
that there is more than one dimension; they disagree on how many. The ratifier may wish to cite Ryff
for the theoretical proposal and Springer & Hauser / Abbott for the empirical count.
