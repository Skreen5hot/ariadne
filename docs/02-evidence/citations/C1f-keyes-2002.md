  Construct: mental health operationalised as a syndrome of symptoms of positive feelings and positive

  Effect: risk of a major depressive episode about two times higher among languishing than moderately
  healthy adults and nearly six times higher than among flourishing adults. Men, older adults, the
  more educated and the married were more likely to be mentally healthy.
  Factor evidence: none reported in the abstract; see replication sources for CFA of the three-domain
