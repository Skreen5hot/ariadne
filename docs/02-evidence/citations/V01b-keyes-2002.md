Graded Moderate: partial support on a replicated finding I opened (three-factor and two-factor
models beat a single factor in Lamers et al. 2011, replicated in a validation half-sample). The
V01 clause "cannot be reduced to single measures" is the weak point; the factors correlate .30-.60
and are routinely summed. The retraction check is the Europe PMC record, which carries no
retraction or correction flag. Table 2 in the Lamers PDF extracted with misaligned columns; the fit
values quoted are those I could assign to the three-factor models with confidence (RMSEA and CFI);
the chi-square values are omitted for that reason.
