Abstract-only on a 1995 narrative (not systematic) review. Graded Moderate because the direction it
supports (belonging and relatedness predict wellbeing) is confirmed by meta-analyses opened here;
the claim's superlative and its "cultural frameworks" clause are outside this source. Note that
Baumeister and Leary's review is not itself independent of the SDT relatedness literature in
spirit, though the meta-analyses above are by other groups.
