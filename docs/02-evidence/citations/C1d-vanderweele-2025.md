## Notes
Graded "Argued only" under the rule that a presupposition is not a finding: this paper assumes the six
domains. Wave 1 is a single study; no independent replication exists yet (later waves are planned). If
the register wants empirical support for distinct domains from this dataset, a companion Special
Collection paper reporting domain-level divergence would be the right citation.
