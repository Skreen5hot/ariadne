fit_note: |
  Supports "mystical experience can be measured reliably and has stable dimensions (Stace's
  introvertive/extrovertive phenomenology plus an interpretation factor) and lawful correlates". It is
  not evidence that transcendent experiences are transformative, and it is silent on the first clause of
  the v1 sentence. The three-factor structure was confirmed in Hood et al. 2001 (opened in full), which
  also reports that the introvertive factor predicted psychological dysfunction in both US and Iranian
  samples, a point that complicates any straightforward "transformative for the better" reading. The
  closest empirical support for "transformative" that I found is the psilocybin work of Griffiths et al.
  2006 (see V12bx), which uses Hood's M-Scale as an outcome measure.
