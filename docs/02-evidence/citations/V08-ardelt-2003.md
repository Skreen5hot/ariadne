## Notes
This citation appears to have been chosen for the wisdom construct and measure, not for the wellbeing
finding. The 3D-WS is the standard instrument; the "independent of other factors" claim comes from
Ardelt's earlier 1997 SEM study (V08x). If the ratifier wants a 3D-WS-based test of the wellbeing claim,
the full 2003 paper (pp. 275-324) or later 3D-WS studies would need to be opened; none was accessible here.
