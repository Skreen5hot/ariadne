fit_note: |
  The claim has two conjuncts. (1) 'Reflective capacity predicts better life outcomes' is NOT supported as an
  unconditional claim: the review's central evidence is that introspection is of limited use and that two
  common forms of it (reasons analysis, rumination) worsen judgment and mood; benefit is confined to forms that
  build a coherent narrative (expressive writing). (2) 'Self-knowledge improves life outcomes' is the authors'
  considered view, but they call the wellbeing evidence limited, cite only a few correlational studies on
  implicit-explicit congruence, and set the positive-illusions literature against it. Net: partial support for
  the second conjunct, and evidence against the first as worded.
